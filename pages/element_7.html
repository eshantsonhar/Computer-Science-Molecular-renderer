N - Nitrogen

Example: Ammonia (NH₃)

Element Information

Atomic Number: 7

Group: 15

Period: 2

Category: nonmetal

Example Molecule

NH₃

A crucial industrial chemical used in fertilizers, cleaning products, and as a refrigerant.

Element Facts

Nitrogen makes up 78% of Earth's atmosphere by volume and is essential for amino acids and proteins.
