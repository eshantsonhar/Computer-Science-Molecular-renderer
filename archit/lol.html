Skeletal Controls

• Left-Click canvas to spawn/extend chains.
• Click an existing bond line to upgrade it to a Double/Triple bond.

• Shift + Left-Click a bond line to decrease or delete it.
• Shift + Left-Click an atom node to delete it entirely.
